    Frequently Asked Questions

    Common questions about business loans, eligibility, process, and documentation.

    AADS Financial Services acts as a facilitator, helping customers explore business loan options from partner banks and NBFCs based on their requirements.

    A business loan is a financial product offered by banks and NBFCs to support business needs such as working capital, expansion, or equipment purchase.

    Common documents include identity proof (PAN/Aadhaar), address proof, business proof (GST/Udyam), bank statements, and financial documents. Requirements may vary by lender.

    Eligibility depends on factors such as business vintage, turnover, credit profile, and lender policies. Both new and existing businesses may apply, subject to eligibility.

    Unsecured business loan options may be available without collateral, depending on your eligibility and lender criteria.

    Processing time may vary depending on the lender, documentation, and verification process. It typically ranges from a few days to longer, based on individual cases.

    Loan amounts vary depending on business profile, financials, and lender policies. You can explore options ranging from smaller to higher ticket sizes.

    EMI depends on loan amount, tenure, and interest rate. Final EMI details are provided by the lender after approval.
